# Industrial Investment News Verification

Use for briefs about facilities, industrial parks, logistics, project approvals, employment claims, and policy incentives.

## Project status controls the wording

| Source wording | Supported wording | Unsupported leap |
|---|---|---|
| Approved or cleared | Approved project or cleared proposal | Operating facility |
| Foundation stone or groundbreaking | Project under development | Current production |
| Commissioned or commercial operations begun | Commissioned facility or operating unit | Every programme phase completed |
| Employment potential or expected jobs | Projected jobs or employment potential | Jobs already created |
| Projected capacity | Planned capacity | Current output |

A past approval does not prove that investment is currently being deployed. Check for a newer status before describing present activity.

## Research order

1. Find the issuer's release, filing, clearance notice, department order, policy document, or official announcement.
2. Confirm the exact project, phase, location, status, and date.
3. Use reputable reporting for corroboration or context. Read the primary source when precise numbers are central.
4. Identify sponsored, partner, promotional, or republished material. Attribute claims found only there or omit them.
5. Check investment, capacity, jobs, location, and partner identity separately. Briefs can combine figures from different phases.

## Pitfall: totals that do not reconcile

Compare baseline, increment, facility total, and company total. Apply the same check to floor area, funding, and phased construction.

Do not add project values into a confirmed programme total unless an issuer confirms the combined scope. If published numbers conflict, describe the claims separately and flag the unexplained difference. Do not invent a repair.

## Policy amendments

- Read the original policy for rate, eligibility, and duration.
- Find amendment notices and effective dates separately.
- Verify against current official docs. A landing page can retain superseded terms.
- Attribute a changed rate to its amendment rather than implying it appeared in the original policy.

## Attribution and scope

- Use `the company said` for issuer claims that lack independent confirmation.
- Keep proposed investment, approved investment, committed funding, and completed expenditure distinct.
- Preserve superlatives and sustainability claims only with clear attribution or appropriate independent evidence.
- Do not treat promotional copy or an outlet's republished release as independent reporting.
- Separate a facility's forecast capacity from measured output and employment potential from payroll.

## Correction log

Use one row per material claim:

`Claim | source URL and project status | supported wording | correction or omission reason`

Carry material corrections and unresolved gaps into the article's publishing notes.
